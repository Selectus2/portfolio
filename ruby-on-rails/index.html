    Ruby gems I maintain

    Both MIT-licensed and framework-agnostic — neither is a Rails engine.

    payu-ruby

    v0.2.0 · MIT · Ruby >= 3.1 · released 19 July 2026

    Ruby client for the PayU India payment gateway — hash generation, payment params, server-to-server verification, refunds. Framework-agnostic; works with Rails, Sinatra, or plain Ruby. Zero runtime dependencies.

    trainers-rb

    v0.1.1 · MIT · Ruby >= 3.1 · released 9 June 2026

    Training loop, LoRA, LR scheduling, callbacks and safetensors serialization for fine-tuning HuggingFace transformer models. Built on torch-rb and transformers-rb. Early — 0.1.x.
