    Murder on the Orient Express

    Agatha Christie · 1934

    Fiction – Global

    A murder mystery solved by the famous detective Hercule Poirot.

    Masterclass in suspense and whodunit.

    Siddhartha

    Hermann Hesse · 1922

    Fiction – Global

    A spiritual journey of self-discovery inspired by the life of Gautama Buddha.

    Philosophical and poetic; a literary gem.

    The Almanack of Naval Ravikant

    Eric Jorgenson · 2020

    Entrepreneurship

    Collection of Naval’s thoughts on wealth, happiness, and decision-making.

    Concise and deeply thought-provoking.

    The Art of War

    Sun Tzu · 5th century BC

    Non-Fiction – Self-Improvement

    Ancient Chinese military treatise on strategy, power, and leadership.

    Timeless and applicable to life and business alike.

    The Lean Startup

    Eric Ries · 2011

    Entrepreneurship

    Introduces the concept of building products using lean methodology.

    A game-changer for modern startups.

    The Psychology of Money

    Morgan Housel · 2020

    Non-Fiction – Self-Improvement

    Explains money behavior and decision-making with relatable stories.

    Insightful, non-preachy, and very readable.

    Zero to One

    Peter Thiel, Blake Masters · 2014

    Entrepreneurship

    Focuses on building unique businesses that create new value.

    Philosophical yet practical startup thinking.

    Pankaj Goyal · 2018

    Entrepreneurship

    A beginner’s manual to prepare for launching your startup.

    Robert Louis Stevenson · 1886

    Fiction – Global

    A Gothic novella about dual personalities and the nature of evil.

    Ajey Zankar · 2016

    Fiction – Marathi

    Retelling of a critical part of the Mahabharata in Marathi prose.

    Hard Times

    Charles Dickens · 1854

    Fiction – Global

    A social critique of utilitarianism and industrial society in Victorian England.

    Classic Dickens—sharp, moral, and melancholic.

    How To Stop Worrying And Start Living

    Dale Carnegie · 1948

    Non-Fiction – Self-Improvement

    A classic guide to managing stress and finding peace of mind.

    Old but gold—practical advice even today.

    Ikigai

    Hector Garcia Puigcerver · 2016

    Non-Fiction – Self-Improvement

    A Japanese philosophy on purpose, longevity, and fulfillment.

    Simple yet impactful insights on meaningful life.

    LVOE

    Poetry – Global

    A collection of love, loss, and healing poetry by the Instagram-famous poet.

    Non-Fiction – Self-Improvement

    Napoleon Hill · 1937

    Non-Fiction – Self-Improvement

    One of the earliest personal development books focused on wealth and mindset.

    Fiction – Indian
